POINT 2 — “Why this recommendation?” Toggle
What it is

A small expandable explanation under the AI result that tells users how the AI reached that suggestion.

Right now your app says:

“Dehydration or Temporary Blood Sugar Imbalance”

That’s good — but users subconsciously think:

“Okay… but WHY?”

If you don’t answer that, trust drops.

What to ADD (Very Simple)

Under the diagnosis title or severity badge, add a small link:

Why am I seeing this?

When clicked (or tapped), it expands and shows something like:

AI reasoning:
Based on symptoms such as dizziness and fatigue, along with common wellness patterns, dehydration and short-term glucose imbalance are possible contributors. This is not a diagnosis.

Why this matters (Big deal)

Builds AI transparency

Prevents “fake AI” suspicion

Makes users feel respected, not talked down to

💡 Apps like Google Health, Apple Health, and Headspace ALL do this.

Design tip

Small font

Muted color

Expand/collapse animation (150ms)

This should feel optional, not forced.

🔹 POINT 3 — Post-Analysis CTA (What Now?)
What’s missing right now

After reading the recommendations, the user hits a dead end.

They think:

“Okay… I read it. Now what?”

That’s bad UX.

What to ADD (Only two or three actions)

At the bottom of the right panel, add gentle next steps, not aggressive buttons.

Best options for your app:

🥗 View nutrition suggestions

💧 Track hydration today

🔄 Check another symptom

Example placement

Below the disclaimer, add a soft button row:

[ View Nutrition Tips ]   [ Check Another Symptom ]


Muted style. Not flashy.

Why this matters

Keeps users inside your app

Turns insight → action

Increases retention massively

Without this, the experience feels unfinished.
